## 🎯 About the Internship

The internship was structured around **7 progressive tasks**, each building on the last, covering data exploration, demographic and correlation analysis, behavioural analysis, and finally a consolidated interactive dashboard — using a dataset on individual investment preferences (investment avenues, savings objectives, duration, monitoring frequency, and information sources).

## ✅ Tasks Completed

| # | Task | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Data Exploration and Summary** | Import the dataset, review structure/data types, and calculate key summary statistics (average age, investment %, common savings objectives). |
| 2 | **Gender-based Analysis** | Compare investment choices (equity, mutual funds, government bonds) across genders. |
| 3 | **Objective Analysis** | Explore the relationship between savings objectives and investment avenues. |
| 4 | **Investment Duration and Frequency** | Visualize investment duration distribution and monitoring-frequency patterns. |
| 5 | **Reasons for Investment** | Identify and visualize the most common reasons for investing. |
| 6 | **Source of Information** | Analyze where individuals get their investment information. |
| 7 | **Combine Insights into a Dashboard** | Bring all visuals together into one polished, interactive Power BI dashboard. |

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** — data modeling & visualization
- **Power Query** — data cleaning & transformation
- **DAX** — calculated columns and measures
- **Dashboard Design** — slicers, cross-filtering, tooltips, theming

## 📈 Key Insights

- Mutual funds and equity are the most widely held investment avenues.
- Investment choice correlates strongly with the stated savings objective (e.g., capital appreciation → equity/mutual funds).
- Monitoring frequency influences risk appetite — frequent monitors lean toward market-linked instruments.
- Better returns and tax benefits are the top-cited reasons for investing.
- The internet and financial consultants are the leading sources of investment information.
